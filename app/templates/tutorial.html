Tutorial

Learn how to use the table and map to explore species prioritization data.

The Table

Explore species data row-by-row with regular-expression search, filtering, sorting, and custom scoring.

Searching & Filtering

Use the search box at the top of the table to filter species by name, family, or any other field using regular expressions. Results update instantly as you type, so you can quickly zero in on the species you're interested in.

Sorting Columns

Click any column header to sort the table. The first click sorts in ascending order, and clicking again reverses the direction. This helps you quickly find the highest-priority species or group by threat category.

Tip: Use the number keys 1-5 to sort the columns more easily.

Adjusting Scoring Weights

Open the sliders panel to customize how each IUCN threat level and DNA sampling status contributes to the priority score. When you change the weights, the priority calculation will update in real-time, allowing you to quickly fine-tune each parameter to match your own personal prioritization tendencies as closely as possible.

The Map

Visualize species distribution and priority across Denmark's hexagonal grid.

Zooming & Panning

Move around the map using the following controls:
Arrowkeys to move or drag the map using your mouse
Zoom in and out using your scroll wheel or touchpad
Shift + Arrowkeys to tilt and pan the map
Use the little compass in the menu to reset tilt or pan.

Color Intensity

Each hexagon's color represents the priority score for species in that area. The priority is somewhat inversely proportional to wavelength, with purple-blue-green colors being low priority while yellow-orange-red colors being high priority.

{# #}

Hovering & Details

Hover your mouse over any hexagon to see a detailed tooltip. Clicking on a cell will display a table of stats related to that cell so you can really dive deep. Try clicking the cells around yourself and see what priorities lie in your area.

Adjusting Weights in Real-Time

Open the sliders panel while viewing the map and drag any weight slider. Watch the hexagon colors shift instantly across the entire map as the scoring function recalculates. This is a powerful way to explore how different prioritization strategies would affect your sampling strategy geographically.

Open the sliders panel while viewing the map and drag any weight slider. The colors automatically update to fit the new weights. Try changing your configuration and watch the colors change in real-time.
